Coffee Snap is a calendar-first coffee journal for iPhone and iPad. Snap your coffee, keep the sticker, remember every cup.

Common questions

How do I get my journal on my iPad (or a new iPhone)?

On each device, sign in to the same iCloud account, open Coffee Snap and turn on Settings → iCloud → Sync with iCloud. Your coffees, stickers and photos appear on the other device after a short while.

Sync is on, but a coffee hasn't shown up on my other device.

Check that both devices are online, signed in to the same iCloud account, and have Sync with iCloud turned on in Coffee Snap. Then open Coffee Snap on the other device; it refreshes when it comes to the front. Photos can take a little longer than the coffee details.

The camera or photo picker doesn't open.

Check iOS Settings → Coffee Snap → Camera / Photos. You can always log a coffee without a photo; it gets a cup placeholder instead.

How do I delete everything?

Open Settings and tap Delete All Data at the bottom of the page, then confirm. Deleting the app also removes its data from that device.
