Coffee Snap is a calendar-first coffee journal for iPhone and iPad. Snap your coffee, keep the sticker, remember every cup.

Common questions

How do I get my journal on my iPad (or a new iPhone)?

iCloud sync requires Coffee Snap Pro. On each device, sign in to the same iCloud account, open Coffee Snap and turn on Settings → iCloud Sync. Your coffees, stickers and photos appear on the other device after a short while.

Sync is on, but a coffee hasn't shown up on my other device.

Check that both devices are online, signed in to the same iCloud account, and have Sync with iCloud turned on in Coffee Snap. Then open Coffee Snap on the other device; it refreshes when it comes to the front. Photos can take a little longer than the coffee details.

I bought Pro but it's locked.

Open Settings in Coffee Snap, tap Unlock Pro, then Restore Purchases. Make sure the device is signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you used to buy.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Coffee Snap → Cancel. After it ends, your journal stays exactly as it is; Pro themes switch back to Oat Latte and iCloud sync turns off, with your data kept on the device.

Does the lifetime purchase expire?

No. It's a one-time purchase that unlocks Pro permanently on all devices signed in with your Apple ID.

The camera or photo picker doesn't open.

Check iOS Settings → Coffee Snap → Camera / Photos. You can always log a coffee without a photo; it gets a cup placeholder instead.

How do I delete everything?

Open Settings → Privacy Policy → Manage Data → Delete All Data, then confirm. Deletions may also sync to other devices through iCloud, including if you enable sync again later. Deleting the app removes its on-device journal but does not automatically delete your iCloud copy or cancel a subscription.

Which sharing options are free?

Open Share from Home to export a monthly card at 3:4. Calendar is free. Classic, Hand-drawn and Vintage require Pro. Pro also lets you hide the Coffee Snap name and logo using Remove Branding. It does not remove trademarks or objects inside your photos.

Which themes are included?

Oat Latte is the free default theme. Five additional color themes require Pro. Language, appearance and default currency can be selected separately in Settings.

How do monthly reminders work?

Monthly Recap Reminder is free and off by default. Enable it in Settings and allow notifications. When the current month has a record, the app schedules a local reminder for the first day of the next month at 10:00 in the device's local calendar. iOS notification settings and Focus can affect delivery. You can switch it off in Settings.
