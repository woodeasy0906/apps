Coffee Snap – Coffee Tracker

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026 · Developer: HK Time Culture Communication Limited

Coffee Snap is a personal coffee journal for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what the app does with your information. We do not operate a journal or photo server or receive your journal files through normal app use. Your journal stays on your device and, if you enable sync, in your private iCloud account, unless you choose to export or share it, or send it to support. We do not sell your journal or photos.

Optional usage analytics and crash-report sharing are off by default. If you choose to enable Share Usage & Diagnostics in Settings, Firebase processes the technical diagnostics and usage information described below. Separately, Apple and RevenueCat process purchase information needed to provide Coffee Snap Pro. These services use installation or app-user identifiers; this information is separate from your coffee journal.

The optional collection controls described here apply from Coffee Snap 1.0 (build 10). Sharing is off by default on each device, including when upgrading from an earlier build, and your choice is stored on that device until you change it. Earlier TestFlight builds, including build 9, enabled Firebase diagnostics and usage analytics by default and did not offer this switch. The treatment of previously queued transmissions is explained below.

What Coffee Snap stores

When you log a coffee, the app saves what you enter: date and time, coffee type, an optional label, size, rating, price and currency, historically saved shop names, caffeine, sugar, notes, and a favorite mark. If you add a photo, the app stores the original photo and the sticker it makes from it.

Your journal is stored in Coffee Snap's own storage area on your device. Coffee Snap has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own containing your journal. Optional iCloud sync uses your own iCloud account, as described below.

What Coffee Snap does not do

  • It does not send your coffee records, notes, shops, prices or photos anywhere except your own iCloud, if you turn on sync, or a destination you explicitly choose through the system share sheet.
  • Coffee Snap does not display advertising or request permission to track you across other companies' apps or websites. Ad personalization and ad-network registration are disabled in the app configuration.
  • It does not read your contacts, calendar or health data, request device-location permission, or access precise GPS location. Service requests can reveal IP-derived approximate location as described below.
  • It does not access your photo library beyond the photos you explicitly pick.
  • Sticker extraction runs on your device using Apple's Vision and Core Image frameworks. Your photos are not sent to a remote AI service to make stickers.

Local reminders and review preferences

Monthly recap reminders are scheduled on your device from the dates of your saved entries. Reminder preferences, the choice to hide Coffee Snap branding on exports, the diagnostics-sharing choice, and limited review-invitation eligibility information are stored locally, outside your journal's iCloud database.

The app may request Apple's native rating dialog. Apple controls whether it appears and handles any rating you choose to submit. Coffee Snap does not receive a private copy of your rating.

Sharing

If you use Share on a recap or month card, iOS shows the standard share sheet. What happens next is up to you and the app or service you choose. Coffee Snap does not post your journal to a public feed or send it to another service on its own.

iCloud sync: optional

With Coffee Snap Pro, optional iCloud sync can keep your journal the same on devices signed in to your iCloud account. This is off by default and you turn it on in Settings → iCloud Sync.

When it is on, your coffee records, stickers and original photos are stored in the private CloudKit database of your own iCloud account. They do not pass through a server run by us; we cannot read, see or recover your journal. Turning sync off keeps your on-device journal and does not automatically delete the existing iCloud copy. Data stored in iCloud is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Purchases: Coffee Snap Pro

Coffee Snap Pro is sold through Apple's App Store as a monthly or yearly subscription or a one-time purchase. Payment is handled by Apple; we do not receive your payment-card details or Apple ID. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Purchases are subject to Apple's Licensed Application End User License Agreement.

Coffee Snap does not use them for cross-app advertising tracking. RevenueCat keeps purchase information for as long as needed to honour your purchase and meet applicable obligations.

Device backups

Because your journal lives on your device, it is included in your normal device backups, such as iCloud Backup or a computer backup, according to your own iOS settings. Those backups are governed by Apple's privacy policy, not by Coffee Snap.

Deleting your data

  • In Coffee Snap, open Settings → Privacy Policy → Manage Data → Delete All Data to remove coffee entries and photos from the app, after confirmation. Deletions may also sync to your other devices through iCloud, including if sync is enabled again later. This cannot be undone.
  • Deleting the app from your device removes its on-device journal but does not automatically delete your iCloud copy or cancel a subscription. You can manage the iCloud copy in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage.

There is no journal data to delete on our side, because we do not receive it. Deleting your journal or turning off diagnostics sharing does not automatically erase provider-held diagnostics or purchase records. Contact us about those records; we may need information sufficient to locate them and verify your request. Applicable retention and legal obligations may affect deletion.

Children

Coffee Snap is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can assess and address it.

Your rights

Laws such as the GDPR (EU/UK) and the CCPA (California) give you rights over personal data that companies hold about you. We do not operate a server containing your coffee journal. Support correspondence and the technical and purchase information described above are handled separately.

Technical identifiers and purchase records may be personal data under applicable law. You can contact us to request access, correction or deletion, or to raise a privacy concern, subject to applicable requirements. Your journal remains under your control on your device and in your own iCloud account.

Changes

If a future version of Coffee Snap changes these practices, we will update the published policy and its effective date before that version is released.

Support correspondence

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include, and use it to respond to your request. Please avoid sending passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information.
