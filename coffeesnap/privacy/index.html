Coffee Snap – Coffee Journal

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026 · Developer: HK Time Culture Communication Limited

Coffee Snap is a personal coffee journal for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: everything you record stays on your device, or in your own iCloud account if you turn on sync. Coffee Snap does not collect, upload, sell or share your journal or your photos. To keep the app working we receive crash reports and anonymous usage statistics, and the purchase status needed to unlock Coffee Snap Pro. None of it is linked to you, and all of it is described below.

What Coffee Snap stores

When you log a coffee, the app saves what you enter: date and time, coffee type, an optional label, size, rating, price and currency, shop name, caffeine, sugar, notes, and a favorite mark. If you add a photo, the app stores the original photo and the sticker it makes from it.

All of this is stored only on your device, in Coffee Snap's own storage area. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Coffee Snap has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own. Optional iCloud sync uses your own iCloud account (see below).

What Coffee Snap does not do

  • It does not send your coffee records, notes, shops, prices or photos anywhere except your own iCloud (if you turn on sync).
  • It does not use advertising SDKs or the advertising identifier (IDFA), and it does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • It does not read your contacts, location, calendar or health data.
  • It does not access your photo library beyond the photos you explicitly pick.

Permissions the app may ask for

PermissionWhy
CameraOnly when you tap Take Photo, to photograph your coffee. Photos are saved inside the app on your device.
Photos (choose a photo)When you tap Choose from Photos, iOS shows a picker and Coffee Snap receives only the photo you select. It cannot see the rest of your library.
Photos (add only)Only when you tap Save Image on a recap or month card, so the app can add that one image to your library. Coffee Snap cannot read, edit or delete anything in your library.

You can change any of these permissions later in the iOS Settings app. Denying them does not stop you from logging coffees without photos.

Sharing

If you use Share on a recap or month card, iOS shows the standard share sheet. What happens next is up to you and the app or service you choose. Coffee Snap itself does not post or send anything.

iCloud sync (optional)

Coffee Snap can keep your journal the same on all devices signed in to your iCloud account. This is off by default and you turn it on in Settings → iCloud → Sync with iCloud. When it is on, your coffee records, stickers and original photos are stored in the private CloudKit database of your own iCloud account, which only you can access. They never pass through a server run by us: we cannot read, see or recover your journal, and turning sync off keeps everything on the device. Data stored in iCloud is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Crash reports and usage statistics

Coffee Snap Pro is sold through Apple's App Store as a monthly or yearly subscription or a one-time purchase. Payment is handled by Apple; we never see your payment details, name or Apple ID. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Purchases are subject to Apple's Licensed Application End User License Agreement.

Device backups

Because your journal lives on your device, it is included in your normal device backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) according to your own iOS settings. Those backups are governed by Apple's privacy policy, not by Coffee Snap.

Deleting your data

  • In Coffee Snap, open Settings → Delete All Data to remove every coffee and photo from the app.
  • Deleting the app from your device also removes all of its data on that device. If iCloud sync is on, the copy in your iCloud account can be removed in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage.

There is no journal data to delete on our side, because we never receive it. The crash, usage and purchase records described above are not linked to you, so we generally cannot find the ones that belong to a specific person; if you have a request about them, contact us at the address below and we will help within 30 days.

Children

Coffee Snap is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. The app collects no personal information from any user.

Your rights

Laws such as the GDPR (EU/UK) and the CCPA (California) give you rights over personal data that companies hold about you. Coffee Snap holds no record of who you are or what you logged. The crash, usage and purchase records above are not linked to you personally; you can still contact us about them. Everything else is under your control on your own device and iCloud account.

Changes

If a future version of Coffee Snap starts doing something different, we will update this page and the effective date above before that version is released.
