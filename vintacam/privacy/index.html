Vinta Cam – Retro Film Camera

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026 · Developer: HK Time Culture Communication Limited

Vinta Cam is a camera app. Your photos stay on your device. We do not have user accounts and we do not upload your photos. The third-party services described below process limited purchase data and, only if you opt in, technical and usage analytics data. This page explains what data is used and why.

Your photos

  • Photos you take or import are processed on your device and saved to the app's own library on your device and, with your permission, to the iOS Photos app.
  • Vinta Cam does not upload your photos to us or to our analytics or purchase providers. Apple may back up or sync photos and app data through iCloud according to your own device and Apple account settings.
  • Deleting a photo inside Vinta Cam does not delete the copy in the Photos app, and vice versa.

Permissions the app asks for

PermissionWhy

Apple and RevenueCat process the information needed to provide and restore Pro purchases. Google Firebase Analytics is optional and remains off until you enable it. None of these services receives your photos from Vinta Cam.

Vinta Cam does not ask you to create an account or provide your name, email address, or Apple ID to these services. Analytics uses an app instance identifier and, under the current SDK configuration, the Identifier for Vendor (IDFV) to distinguish app installations and devices. Usage and technical data can therefore be associated with an installation or device; a random identifier does not mean that all data is fully anonymous. RevenueCat uses a random app user identifier to maintain purchase and entitlement records. The app does not request access to the advertising identifier (IDFA) or ask for permission to track across other apps.

Optional usage analytics and your choice

Usage analytics is off by default, including when updating from a version that did not offer this choice. Firebase is not configured while this choice is off at startup. You can enable or withdraw consent in Vinta Cam: camera list > More > Share Usage Analytics. Core shooting, imports, local saving and Pro purchases do not require this optional consent.

Turning the setting off stops future analytics collection and asks the SDK to clear local analytics data and reset its app instance identifier. This does not undo requests that were already sent or automatically delete data already held by a provider. You may contact us to request deletion of previously collected data. The current app no longer includes automatic Firebase Crashlytics reporting; reports sent by earlier versions are subject to the retention and deletion process below.

Advertising storage, advertising user data and personalized advertising consent remain disabled, even if you enable usage analytics. We do not use your photos or photo metadata for analytics or advertising.

We require our service providers to protect data consistently with this policy and applicable App Store privacy requirements. Their data-protection information is available from Google Firebase and RevenueCat's Data Processing Addendum. These disclosures do not replace our responsibility for the app's data practices.

Data we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number, or account of any kind
  • No uploads of your photos or their metadata; photo processing and reading capture dates stay on your device
  • No contacts or precise/GPS location. We do not request location permission. The approximate IP-derived region used by Analytics is described above

Purchases

Pro is sold through Apple's App Store as a subscription or a one-time purchase. Payment is handled by Apple; we never see your payment details. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled in your iOS Settings under your Apple ID. Purchases are subject to Apple's Licensed Application End User License Agreement.

Data retention and your rights

Retention periods depend on the type of data, the provider's practices, and the applicable service configuration; we do not promise a single fixed retention period for all historical crash or usage data. Purchase and entitlement records are retained as needed to honour and restore your purchase. Because Vinta Cam has no user accounts, your name or email alone may not identify records associated with a particular app installation or device. If you have a request about your data, including deletion, contact us at the address below and we will help within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR (EU/UK) or the CCPA (California) to access, correct, or delete personal data. You can exercise these rights by contacting us.

Children

Vinta Cam is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's update notes.
