简体中文 · Privacy Policy

GolfCut edits your golf videos on your iPhone. Make a single swing highlight, tell the story of one hole, or assemble a round vlog with scorecards. It requires iOS 18 or later.

Need a sample to try the editor?

Download the original illustrated practice clip (8 seconds, about 1.1 MB, MP4 with sound). This is an illustration, not camera footage or an example of automatic tracking. Save the video to Photos with the iOS share sheet, then choose it in GolfCut's photo picker. No account is needed.

How do I start?

Choose One Swing, One Hole or Round Vlog. For a hole project, set Par 3, 4 or 5, then import your original video clips with the system photo picker. Tap a clip to edit it. Scores are entered separately; the number of imported videos is not your score.

How do trim and ball paths work?

In Trim, move the playhead to locate a frame; the left and right handles set the kept range. Use the frame buttons for precision. Manual Ball Trace lets you select the ball at takeoff and adjust the curve's start, control point and landing point. It is an illustrative overlay, not automatic ball tracking or a measurement of speed or distance.

How do I save and export?

Apply a tool to keep its adjustment in the working project. When leaving a project, choose Save to preserve it for next time. Preview your selected scope, then generate a 9:16, 1080 × 1920 MP4. Generation saves the project first. Save the finished video to Photos or use the share sheet; opening the share sheet does not itself publish anything.

A video will not import or export.

Make sure the selected video has downloaded from iCloud Photos and can play in Photos. Individual source clips must be between 0.5 seconds and 3 minutes; trim a longer source in Photos first. Total output is limited to 20 minutes. Keep GolfCut in the foreground during generation and check available device storage.

Where are my projects?

Saved projects appear on Home. Data is stored locally and is not synced by GolfCut. Delete App removes its local data; Offload App retains documents. Deleting a project removes its library record, while local media copies and earlier exports may remain until the app is deleted. Original videos and exports saved to Photos are managed separately.

Can I use another language?

GolfCut follows your system's preferred supported language: English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, German, French, Spanish, Italian or Brazilian Portuguese. Change your preferred languages in iOS Settings. Unsupported languages fall back to a supported language, with English as the default.

Are there subscriptions?

GolfCut 1.0 has no subscriptions, in-app purchases, account or advertisements. Its editing tools and exports do not require a purchase.

联系与支持

GolfCut 支持 iOS 18 及以上的 iPhone，可制作一杆高光、单洞视频和整场 Vlog。联系客服：support@itimeculture.com。请说明设备型号、系统版本和遇到的步骤，不必附上私人素材。

示例素材

可以下载原创插画练习样片（8 秒、约 1.1 MB、含声音的 MP4），通过 iOS 分享菜单保存视频到相册，再从 GolfCut 的相册选择器导入。它是示意动画，不是真实拍摄，也不代表自动球路识别，无需账号即可使用。

开始编辑

在首页选择制作方式。球洞项目先设置 Par 3、4 或 5，再导入原始片段，点击片段进入编辑。Score 为独立填写的成绩，不由视频数量推算。

裁剪与手动球路

播放指针仅定位当前帧，两端手柄设置保留范围，可用上一帧和下一帧按钮微调。手动球路从击球帧开始，由你点选球的位置并调整起点、曲线控制点和落点；它是示意效果，不是自动追踪，也不测量球速或距离。

保存与生成

应用工具调整后，离开项目时选择保存，下次即可从首页继续。预览后生成 9:16、1080 × 1920 的 MP4，再选择保存到相册或分享。生成前会先保存项目。打开分享面板不代表视频已经发布。

导入或生成失败

请确认素材已从 iCloud 下载且能在相册中播放。单段原素材须为 0.5 秒至 3 分钟，过长可先在相册裁剪；成片最长 20 分钟。生成时保持 App 在前台，并检查设备剩余空间。

项目、语言与费用

项目保存在本机，GolfCut 不提供云同步。删除 App 会删除本地数据；删除项目只移除项目库条目，部分媒体副本可能保留。相册原视频和已保存成片需另行管理。App 自动跟随系统的受支持语言，支持简繁中文、英、日、韩、德、法、西班牙、意大利和巴西葡萄牙语。GolfCut 1.0 不含订阅、内购、账号或广告。
