Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026 · Applies to GolfCut 1.0

简体中文 · Support

GolfCut is an on-device golf video editor for iPhone, operated by HK Time Culture Communication Limited. This version has no account, advertisements, in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Information stays on your device

We do not collect or receive your videos, project names, scores, ball-path annotations, editing settings, identifiers, usage analytics or diagnostic reports through GolfCut. GolfCut does not include third-party analytics, advertising, purchase or remote AI services. Video editing and rendering take place on your device.

Photos and local storage

You choose videos using Apple's system photo picker. GolfCut receives the videos you select, not general access to your photo library. If a selected video is stored in iCloud Photos, Apple may download it according to your Apple account and device settings. GolfCut keeps working copies, project records, recovery checkpoints and exported videos in its local app storage.

When you choose Save to Photos, GolfCut requests permission to add the exported video to your photo library. You can decline this permission and still use editing and the system share sheet. Change the permission in iOS Settings at any time. GolfCut does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location or tracking permission.

Sharing, backups and external links

GolfCut only opens the system share sheet when you choose to share a video. The destination you select handles that copy under its own privacy policy. GolfCut does not automatically publish videos. iOS device backups and iCloud Photos may store your data according to your Apple settings; GolfCut does not run a separate cloud-sync service.

Opening our policy or support website connects your browser to our GitHub Pages hosting provider. Ordinary website requests may expose your IP address and browser information to that provider. These pages have no advertising, analytics scripts or tracking cookies. Apple's standard terms link opens Apple's website.

Retention and deletion

Deleting a project or clip removes its entry and editing records from the project library. Local media copies and earlier exports may remain in app storage. To remove all GolfCut app data, use Delete App in iOS Settings; Offload App retains documents. Backups are managed separately in Apple's settings. Original videos and exports already saved to Photos, Files or another destination are not deleted by GolfCut and must be managed there.

Support and policy changes

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include, and use it to respond to your request. Please avoid sending private videos unless they are necessary to explain a problem. You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing support@itimeculture.com, subject to any applicable legal retention duties. We do not sell your support information.

GolfCut is a general-purpose editing tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. If a future version changes how data is handled, we will update this policy and provide any required notices or choices.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 8 日 · 适用于 GolfCut 1.0

GolfCut 是由 HK Time Culture Communication Limited 提供的 iPhone 高尔夫视频编辑工具。本版本无需账号，不含广告、应用内购买或订阅。

数据在本机处理

GolfCut 不向开发者收集或传输你的视频、项目名称、成绩、球路标注、编辑参数、标识符、使用统计或诊断报告。App 未接入第三方分析、广告、支付或远程 AI 服务，编辑与视频生成均在设备本地完成。

相册与本地存储

你通过 Apple 系统照片选择器主动选择视频，GolfCut 只能接收选中的素材，并不获得整个相册的读取权限。若素材位于 iCloud 照片，Apple 可能按你的账号和设备设置下载素材。GolfCut 会在 App 本地存储中保存素材副本、项目记录、恢复检查点和导出视频。

只有在你选择“保存到相册”时，App 才申请向相册添加视频的权限。拒绝后仍可使用编辑和系统分享功能。你可以在 iOS 设置中更改权限。GolfCut 不申请相机、麦克风、通讯录、定位或跟踪权限。

分享、备份和外部链接

只有你主动分享时，GolfCut 才打开系统分享面板；你选定的平台按其隐私政策处理该副本。App 不会自动发布视频。系统设备备份或 iCloud 照片可能按你的 Apple 设置保存数据，GolfCut 本身不提供独立的云同步服务。

打开隐私政策或支持网页时，浏览器会连接 GitHub Pages 托管服务，普通网页请求可能向该服务提供方暴露 IP 地址和浏览器信息。网页不含广告、分析脚本或跟踪 Cookie。Apple 标准使用条款链接会打开 Apple 网站。

保留与删除

删除项目或片段会移除项目库中的条目及其编辑记录；素材副本和此前导出的视频可能继续保留在 App 存储中。若需清除 GolfCut 全部本地数据，请在 iOS 设置中选择“删除 App”；“卸载 App”会保留文稿。系统备份需在 Apple 设置中另行管理。相册、文件或其他平台中已有的原视频和成片不受影响，请到相应位置自行管理。

客服与政策变更

当你发邮件联系客服时，我们会接收你的邮箱和主动提供的信息，仅用于处理请求。除非排查问题确有需要，请勿发送私人视频。你可通过 support@itimeculture.com 请求删除客服往来资料；法律要求保留的情况除外。我们不出售这些信息。

GolfCut 是通用编辑工具，不通过 App 主动收集儿童个人信息。如果后续版本改变数据处理方式，我们会更新政策，并提供适用的告知与选择。
